# Vyasen

### Connecting the world of knowledge.

Inspired by the legacy of Ved Vyasa, Vyasen is an open-source initiative building modern infrastructure for connected education and institutional ecosystems.

We believe technology should make it easier for people, educators and institutions to connect, collaborate and grow.

## What we're building

- **Unified identity:** A global identity across multiple institutions.
- **Institutional workspaces:** Independent, secure environments for schools, colleges and universities.
- **Modular architecture:** Extensible systems designed to evolve.
- **Open infrastructure:** Technology that institutions and developers can adopt, extend and self-host.

## Our vision

One identity. Connected institutions. A world of knowledge.

We're building in the open. Explore our repositories, follow our progress and contribute to the ecosystem.
